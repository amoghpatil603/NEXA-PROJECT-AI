# NEXA User Guide

## Getting Started
Welcome to NEXA v1.0.0. NEXA provides advanced multi-agent conversational reasoning, autonomous workflow execution, and secure tool usage.

## Interacting with Agents
- Simply enter your objective or question in natural language.
- The Reasoning Engine automatically selects the optimal route (Direct Model, RAG, Memory, Tool, or Multi-Agent coordination).
- View confidence scores and concise explanations for every response.
