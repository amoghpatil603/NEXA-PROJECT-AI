# NEXA Platform - User Guide

## Introduction
NEXA is your multi-modal, highly capable AI assistant. It sees, hears, remembers, and acts.

## Interacting with the AI
- **Text**: Type naturally into the input box on the Web or Mobile app.
- **Voice**: Click the microphone icon to speak. The system will transcribe your audio and the AI will respond.
- **Vision**: Upload images or PDFs using the attachment button. The AI will automatically analyze the contents of the image/document and include it in its reasoning.

## Platform Features
- **Memory**: The AI will proactively remember important details from your conversation. You do not have to explicitly command it to remember.
- **Mobile Access**: Log in on the mobile app to continue the exact same session on the go.

## Tips
- Be specific with complex requests to allow the Multi-Agent framework to correctly delegate sub-tasks.
- If you notice unexpected behavior, try clearing your browser cache or restarting the mobile app.
